# ShopMy find a product and its rate
trigger: shopmy link for, make a shopmy link, commission rate for, find on shopmy, affiliate link for, is this brand on shopmy, best rate on shopmy, shopmy rate

1. goto https://shopmy.us and open the dashboard; if a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`. Open the "Links" tab and search the product name first: if a link already exists, `read` its retailer and rate and click "copy" instead of making a new one.
2. Otherwise click "add new" (or "create a new link"). For a product name choose "search our catalog", type the name, filter by brand or retailer website if the list is long, and `read` the top 10 results with retailer and commission rate. For a retailer URL choose "Add By URL", paste it, `wait` for the title to load and `read` the retailer and rate shown in the top left.
3. Click "see other retailers" (or the "Found X better rate(s)" note) and `read` every retailer with its rate. Brand Partners pay custom rates and may gift or offer bonuses; network brands (ShareASale, Impact, Rakuten, CJ) pay the network rate.
4. Open the best-paying retailer's product page with tab new, `read` price and stock, tab close.
5. Only if the task asks for the link: click "Create Link" / "Add Product", then "copy" it from the Links tab. Do not add it to a collection or post. Otherwise press Escape and leave nothing created.
6. Report: product, best retailer, price, in stock or not, commission rate, the ShopMy link if created, and the alternatives as a table (retailer, rate, price). If the brand is not commissionable, say so and name the closest commissionable retailer.
