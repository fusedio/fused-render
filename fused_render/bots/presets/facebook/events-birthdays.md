# Facebook events, invites and birthdays
trigger: facebook events, upcoming events on facebook, facebook invites, facebook birthdays, whose birthday, events this weekend on facebook, event invitations

1. goto https://www.facebook.com/events/calendar/ (Your events). On a sign-in page use `login`. `read` the list: event name, date and time, place, your status (Going, Interested, Invited).
2. Click the left-menu filters in turn and `read` each: Going, Interested, Invites (still unanswered), Hosting. Skip Past unless asked.
3. goto https://www.facebook.com/events/birthdays/ and `read` the Today, Recent and Upcoming sections: names and dates.
4. For events the task cares about (max 3) open the event and `read`: host, date, address or online link, description, Going/Interested counts, ticket link. Never click Going, Interested or Invite.
5. For discovery ("what's on this weekend") goto https://www.facebook.com/events/ , click the date chip (Today, Tomorrow, This week, This weekend) and the Local toggle, then `read` the first 10 cards: name, date, place, interested count.
6. Report three lists: Your events (date, name, place, status, link https://www.facebook.com/events/<id>/), Invites waiting for an answer, Birthdays this week. Nothing was RSVP'd. Over 15 events: `save` facebook-events.md.
