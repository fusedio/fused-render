# TikTok trends via Creative Center
trigger: tiktok trends, trending hashtags on tiktok, trending sounds on tiktok, tiktok creative center, trending songs on tiktok, what's trending on tiktok, tiktok trend report

1. goto https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/popular/hashtag/pc/en . It works without login; ignore the Log in button top-right and accept the cookie banner.
2. Set the filters above the table: Region (defaults to the current country; change it if the task names one), Industry when the task names one, the time frame selector (Last 7 days / 30 days / 120 days), and the "New to top 100" toggle for newcomers only.
3. `read` the table: rank, hashtag, number of posts, trend line direction. Click "See more" at the bottom once for 40 rows.
4. For the 3 hashtags the task cares about click "See analytics" and `read` the detail page (https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/hashtag/<name>/pc/en): posts, views, the trend over time, related hashtags, audience age and region.
5. For sounds use the Songs tab (https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/popular/music/pc/en , sub-tabs Popular and Breakout, filter "Approved for business use"); for people the Creators tab (.../popular/creator/pc/en , sortable by Followers, Engagement, Popularity); for examples the Videos tab.
6. Report a table of the top 15 hashtags (rank, hashtag, posts, trend, analytics link), then the 3 deep-dives, then the top 5 songs when asked. State the region and time frame used. `save` tiktok-trends.md when the task wants the full list.
