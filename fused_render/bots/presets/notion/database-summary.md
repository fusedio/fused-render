# Notion database summary
trigger: notion database, summarize the board, notion tracker, notion table, notion tasks, what's in the database, notion kanban, notion roadmap

1. Open the database page from the task (its URL, or Search: Control+k, type the name, Enter). If a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`.
2. The view tabs sit at the top left of the database (Table, Board, Timeline, Calendar, List, Gallery, Chart). If a table view exists, click it: a table shows every property as a column. Do not add views, filters or sorts and do not edit any cell.
3. `read` the "Filter" and "Sort" chips under the tabs so you can say what the open view already excludes.
4. Scroll to the bottom until the "Load more" link is gone, then `read` the table: name, status, owner or assignee, dates, priority, tags.
5. Count rows by status and by owner; list rows whose date is past and whose status is not done, and rows with no owner or no date. If the task names specific rows, open at most 5 (hover the row, click "Open") and `read` their body.
6. Report: the totals as a short table (status x count, owner x count), the overdue and unowned lists with a link to each row (copy the URL after opening it), the filter the view applies, and the database link. More than 30 rows asked for? `save` the full list as notion-<database>.csv and keep the chat to the counts and the overdue list.
