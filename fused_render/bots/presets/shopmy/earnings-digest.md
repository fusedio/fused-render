# ShopMy earnings digest
trigger: shopmy earnings, my commissions, how much did i make on shopmy, shopmy stats, shopmy orders, shopmy performance, affiliate earnings this week, shopmy payout

1. goto https://shopmy.us and open the dashboard; if a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`. Click the "Links" tab at the top of the page.
2. Set the date range (start and end) to the period the task names (default: last 7 days). In the "My Links" view click the arrow next to "earned" to sort descending, then `read` the first 20 rows: product, retailer, clicks, orders, commission earned, created.
3. Switch to the "Performance by Website" view and `read` it: per retailer domain, order volume, clicks, orders, earnings. Then "All Orders": sale total, commission earned, discount-code flag; note any order with zero commission.
4. Open the "Earnings" tab and `read` the monthly total and referral bonus; then the Payments page ("My Earnings") and `read` Pending, Locked and Paid amounts and "Next Payment" (paid on Fridays once Locked reaches $11).
5. Move the Links date range to the previous period of the same length and `read` the totals again for comparison.
6. If the task asks for an export, click "Download" at the top right of the Links tab; the CSV lands in the Inbox folder.
7. Report: total commission, orders, clicks and order volume with the change versus the previous period; the top 5 links and top 3 retailers by commission; Pending vs Locked vs Paid and the next payout; anything odd (zero-commission orders, a retailer whose rate changed). More than 20 links? `save` the table as shopmy-earnings.csv and keep the chat to the top 5.
