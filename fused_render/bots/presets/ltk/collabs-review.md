# LTK brand collabs and messages
trigger: ltk collabs, ltk brand offers, ltk campaigns, ltk collaboration, brands on ltk want, ltk messages, quick collabs, ltk opportunities

1. goto https://collaborations.shopltk.com/collaborations ("My collaborations" under COLLABORATIONS in the creator dashboard); if a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`.
2. `read` every offer and active collaboration: brand, type (Quick Collab flat fee, campaign), fee, deliverables (posts, platform, product), due date, status (new, accepted, in progress, submitted, paid). Do not click accept, decline, counter or submit.
3. goto https://messaging.shopltk.com ("Messages") and `read` the 10 newest threads: brand, last message, date, whether my reply is pending. Do not reply.
4. goto https://collaborations.shopltk.com/profile and `read` my listed rates, bio and categories so you can say whether an offer is below my minimum. Do not edit.
5. For the top 3 brands by fee, goto https://www.shopltk.com, search the brand and `read` how many recent posts and creators feature it (context only).
6. Report a table sorted by due date: brand, type, fee, deliverables, due date, status, below-my-rate flag, one-line fit note; then the messages needing a reply. Accepting or replying is a separate task that stops at the confirm button.
