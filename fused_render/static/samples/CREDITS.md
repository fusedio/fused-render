# Sample photographs

The three JPEGs here are the base images behind the AI Playground image stage's
edit examples (D467): clicking one of those pills attaches the photo and fills
in the prompt, so a first edit needs neither a file to find nor a sentence to
invent.

All three are Unsplash photographs, used under the [Unsplash
License](https://unsplash.com/license) — free to use, including commercially,
with no permission needed. They were fetched through [Lorem
Picsum](https://picsum.photos), which serves the Unsplash catalogue and reports
each photo's author and source, and re-encoded here at 896×597 (JPEG q82, all
metadata dropped) — small enough to ship, larger than the 640-longest-side the
stage edits at.
