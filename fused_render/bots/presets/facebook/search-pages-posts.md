# Facebook search: pages, people, posts
trigger: search facebook, facebook page for, find on facebook, facebook posts about, facebook page, look up on facebook, does it have a facebook page

1. For a business or organisation goto https://www.facebook.com/search/pages/?q=<name> ; for a person https://www.facebook.com/search/people/?q=<name> ; for public posts on a topic goto https://www.facebook.com/search/posts/?q=<topic> directly (the Posts filter left the search bar in September 2025 but the URL still works). On a sign-in page use `login`.
2. `read` the first 10 results: name, category, followers or likes, location, verified badge, mutual friends for people.
3. Open the top 3 Page matches. `read` the intro box (category, address, hours, phone, website, rating), the About tab at https://www.facebook.com/<page>/about , and the first 5 posts with dates. For a person read only the public header; never send a friend request.
4. For posts search `read` 10 results: author, date, text gist, reactions and comments; tick "Recent posts" in the left filters if present; open at most 3 posts for comments.
5. Report: the matched Page with its facts and link, then posts as bullets newest first with links. Say when a result is region-restricted or shows "content isn't available" (deleted or private).
