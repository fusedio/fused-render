# Google Maps hours and busy times
trigger: opening hours, is it open, what time does it close, when does it open, busy times, how busy is, holiday hours, still open

1. goto https://www.google.com/maps/search/<place name>+<city>/ and open the matching result. Consent dialog → dismiss; captcha → `login`.
2. `read` the panel: the "Open" or "Closed" line with today's closing or opening time; click the hours row to expand the weekly table and `read` it. Note any "Hours might differ" or holiday notice and the "updated by this business" line.
3. Scroll to "Popular times" and `read` its text: Google writes "Usually not too busy", "Usually a little busy", "Usually as busy as it gets" plus a "Live" line; click the day selector for the day the task asks about and read again.
4. If hours are missing or flagged as unconfirmed, read the phone number and website, open the website in a new tab and read its hours page; never call.
5. Report: open now or not and until when, the weekly hours as a short table, busy times for the asked day, any holiday caveat, phone, and the Maps link.
