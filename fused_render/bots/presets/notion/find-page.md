# Notion find a page
trigger: find the notion page, notion page about, where in notion, search notion, notion doc on, in my notion, notion page for, look up in notion

1. goto https://www.notion.so and press Control+k (Cmd+k on Mac) to open Search; type the task's keywords and press Enter. If a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`.
2. `read` the results list: title, parent page or teamspace, last-edited time. If there are more than about 15 hits, narrow with the filters at the top of the search window ("Title only", "Created by", "In" for a parent page, "Date"); switch the sort to "Last edited: Newest first" when the task says recent.
3. Open the best match and `read` the page; scroll to the end first because long pages load as you scroll. Click any "▶" toggle blocks that may hide the answer.
4. If that page does not answer the task, press Escape, reopen Search with Control+k and open the next best match; stop after 3 pages.
5. Copy the chosen page's URL from the address bar (it ends in a 32-character id). If the task also asks who can see it, click "Share" at the top right, `read` the people list and the "General access" value, then press Escape; change nothing.
6. Report: the page that answers the task with the relevant passage quoted exactly, its parent and last-edited time, then the other candidates in one line each, all with links. If the answer is a long section, `save` it as notion-<topic>.md and quote only the key lines in chat.
