# LTK earnings digest
trigger: ltk earnings, my ltk, ltk stats, how did my ltk do, ltk commissions, liketoknowit earnings, ltk analytics, ltk sales this week

1. goto https://creator.shopltk.com/analytics (Analytics under Insights); if a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`. Dismiss any announcement modal.
2. Set the timeframe selector to the period the task names (default: last 7 days) and `read` the summary: commissions, sales or order volume, clicks, orders, conversion rate, returns, and the split by platform (LTK app, social, blog) if shown. Analytics refreshes nightly and clicks every 90 minutes, so today is partial.
3. Scroll to the "Top Performers" table at the bottom. With the dropdown on "Active Links", sort by "Commission" and `read` the top 10 products; then set the dropdown to "Advertisers" and `read` the top 5 retailers by commission; sort by "Clicks" once and note any high-click, zero-order product.
4. Change the timeframe to the previous period of the same length and `read` the summary again for comparison.
5. goto https://creator.shopltk.com/web/affiliate-rewards (Earnings under Insights) and `read` open versus closed commissions, collaboration flat fees if listed, and the most recent payment with its date.
6. Report: commissions, orders, clicks and conversion with the change versus the previous period; top 5 products and top 3 retailers by commission; open vs closed and the last payout; one line on what drove the change. More than 10 products wanted? `save` as ltk-earnings.csv.
