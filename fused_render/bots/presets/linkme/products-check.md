# Linkme products and store check
trigger: linkme products, my linkme store, products on my bio, linkme shop, check my bio products, product blocks, my bio store

1. goto https://linkme.bio/dashboard; if a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`. Open the Products section ("Produtos"), or the product and digital-product blocks inside the Links list if there is no separate section.
2. `read` every product block: name, price, description, destination (external checkout, marketplace, Hotmart, Kiwify, Eduzz, WhatsApp order, PIX card), on or off, schedule. Edit nothing.
3. For the top 8 products, open the destination with tab new, `read` the first screen, compare price and availability with the block, then tab close. Note sold out, price changed, dead link, or a checkout page that errors.
4. goto https://linkme.bio/<username> and `read` which products are visible to the public and in what order.
5. Report a table: product, price on the bio, price at destination, status (ok / price differs / sold out / broken), then suggested fixes ordered by likely lost sales, plus the public page link. More than 15 products? `save` the table as linkme-products.csv.
