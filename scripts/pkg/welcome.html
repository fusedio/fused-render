This installs @DISPLAY_NAME@ @VERSION@ into your Applications folder and opens it when it is done.

The installer is small. During the install it downloads the app itself (about @DMG_SIZE_MB@ MB) from fused.io, checks it, and copies it into Applications, so you need an internet connection, and the “Installing” step can take a few minutes on a slow one.

If a copy of @DISPLAY_NAME@ is already in Applications it is replaced; your projects, settings and bots are kept.

Prefer to do it by hand? The same app ships as a .dmg: open it and drag @DISPLAY_NAME@ onto the Applications folder.
