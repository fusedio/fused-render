# Slack saved items and reminders
trigger: slack saved, saved for later, my slack reminders, slack later, what did i save on slack, my slack to-dos, slack bookmarks

1. goto https://app.slack.com/client and `login` if a sign-in page, workspace picker, 2FA or captcha appears. Click "Later" in the left rail; if it is hidden, open "More" and choose Later.
2. `read` the "In progress" tab: each saved message or file with channel, who posted it, a snippet, and any due date or reminder time.
3. Click the "Archived" and "Completed" tabs only if the task asks for them and `read` the first screen of each.
4. For the 5 newest In progress items, or the ones the task names, click the item to jump to the message in its channel and `read` the messages around it, so the note says what it was about and whether it was resolved. Do not mark anything complete, archive it, or set a reminder.
5. Report a table: what it is (one line), channel, who, saved or due date, status in the conversation (open / answered / done), and the permalink (the href of the message timestamp). Items with due dates first. More than 15 items: `save` as slack-later.md and keep the chat answer to the dated ones.
