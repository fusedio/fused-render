# ShopMy brand opportunities and gifting
trigger: brand offers, gifting opportunities, shopmy collabs, brand opportunities, who wants to work with me, shopmy opportunities, shopmy gifting, lookbook invite, shopmy chat

1. goto https://shopmy.us and open the dashboard; if a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`. Find the Opportunities page in the main navigation (brands' flat-fee bonus campaigns) and `read` every open or invited item: brand, bonus amount, requirement (for example links to share per month, or a post), deadline, status. Do not click accept, apply or decline.
2. Find the Collaborations page (time-bound paid campaigns with briefs) and `read` invitations and in-progress items: brand, fee, deliverables, due date. Do not accept or submit.
3. Find gifting / lookbook invitations (brand partners' invites appear in the dashboard) and `read` brand, products offered, any posting expectation, expiry. Do not request or claim products.
4. Open the brand chat (Chat or Messages) and `read` the 10 newest threads: brand, last message, whether a reply from me is pending. Do not reply.
5. For the top 5 brands by money on the table, search the catalog for the brand and `read` its commission rate and whether it is a Brand Partner.
6. Report a table sorted by deadline: brand, type (Opportunity / Collaboration / Gifting / Chat), what they want, what it pays, deadline, my commission rate with the brand, a one-line fit note; then what needs a reply this week. `remember` the exact page names once seen. Accepting anything is a separate task that stops at the confirm button.
