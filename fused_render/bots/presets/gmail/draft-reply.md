# Gmail reply or draft an email
trigger: reply to the email, draft a reply, respond to the email, write an email to, draft an email, send an email, gmail reply, email them back

1. If an APP TOOL for mail (draft, reply, send) is listed, use it. Otherwise find the thread with a search (from: and subject: from the task, https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#search/<query>) and open it; on a sign-in page use `login`.
2. Click "Expand all" and `read` the whole thread so the reply answers what was asked; note the recipients of the latest message. Default to Reply; use Reply all only when the task says so or the thread is clearly a group discussion.
3. Click Reply (or Reply all) under the last message. For a new email click Compose (top left) and `type` the To and Subject fields.
4. Click the body editor and `type` the text without submit. Keep the user's wording; sign only as the task asks. Ignore the Smart Reply chips and Smart Compose suggestions unless they match exactly.
5. Stop before Send: show the recipients, subject and the exact body as the risky step; proceed only after approval. If the task says "draft only", close the editor with its X (Gmail autosaves to https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#drafts) and send nothing.
6. Report: sent or drafted, to whom, subject, the body, and the thread link.
