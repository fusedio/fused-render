# Instagram post or reel: caption and comments
trigger: this instagram post, instagram reel, comments on instagram, instagram.com/p/, instagram.com/reel/, what are people saying on instagram, summarise this instagram

1. goto the post URL (https://www.instagram.com/p/<code>/ or https://www.instagram.com/reel/<code>/). Logged out shows only the caption and 1-2 comments; on a sign-in page or challenge use `login`, then goto again.
2. `read` the post: account, date, caption with hashtags and mentions, like count (or "liked by X and others"), comment count, the alt text "May be an image of ...". For a carousel click the right arrow and `read` each slide's alt text (max 10). A reel cannot be watched: its caption, the audio title under it and the comments are the content.
3. `read` the comment list. Click the "+" load-more control at the bottom of the comments at most 3 times, and "View replies" on the 3 most-liked comments.
4. Collect up to 15 comments: author, text, like count, time, and whether the poster replied.
5. Report: the post line (account, date, caption gist, counts, link), then what the comments say in 3-4 themes with 5 quoted examples, and the answer to the task's question. Over 20 comments: `save` instagram-comments.md.
