# TikTok search on a topic
trigger: search tiktok, tiktok videos about, tiktoks about, tiktok search, what's on tiktok about, find on tiktok

1. goto https://www.tiktok.com/search?q=<query> (the Top tab). Search needs a logged-in session: on a sign-in sheet, a "Verify to continue" slider captcha or a blank page use `login`, then goto again.
2. `read` the Top results: creator, caption snippet, play count, and the date in the tile corner. Then goto https://www.tiktok.com/search/video?q=<query> for videos only, scroll twice and `read`. For people goto https://www.tiktok.com/search/user?q=<query>.
3. Collect 12 videos: the most viewed, plus the newest when the task says "new" or "this week".
4. Open the top 5: `read` the full caption and hashtags, sound, date, counts, and the top 5 comments. Escape closes the video.
5. If the task has no topic ("what's trending"), goto https://www.tiktok.com/explore , `read` the category chips (Singing & Dancing, Comedy, Sports, Anime & Comics, ...) and the first 12 tiles instead.
6. Report: the recurring hashtags and sounds, the creators who dominate, the angles the videos take (from captions and comments), then 6 bullets with creator, date, caption gist, views/likes, link. Over 10 requested: `save` tiktok-<query>.md and keep the chat to 6.
