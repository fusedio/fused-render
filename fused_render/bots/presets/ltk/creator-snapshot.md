# LTK creator or trend snapshot
trigger: trending on ltk, what's selling on ltk, popular ltk products, ltk trends, what are creators linking, ltk creator page, this ltk shop, compare with ltk creator

1. If the task names a creator, goto https://www.shopltk.com/explore/<handle>; otherwise goto https://www.shopltk.com and open the category page matching the topic (for example /categories/ltkhome, /categories/ltkseasonal, /categories/ltkactive, /categories/ltktravel) or the "Shop the latest posts" feed. No login is needed.
2. Scroll until 20 posts have loaded and `read` them: creator, date, caption, products with retailer and price.
3. Open the 8 most relevant posts (newest, or those matching the task) and `read` the full product list for each; count how often each brand and retailer appears across the 8.
4. For a creator snapshot, also `read` the profile header (bio, follower count if shown) and the dates of the first 20 posts to estimate posts per week.
5. If the task asks which of these fit me, goto https://creator.shopltk.com/analytics (use `login` if asked), `read` the Top Performers "Advertisers" list and match retailers.
6. Report: the top 10 products (product, retailer, price, how many of the 8 posts link it) or, for a creator, their cadence, top brands and price range; which items match my own top retailers; links to each post. More than 10 items wanted? `save` as ltk-trends.md.
