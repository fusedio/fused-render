# Google News follow a developing story
trigger: latest on, what's the latest, updates on the story, follow the story, news about, what happened with, developments on

1. goto https://news.google.com/search?q=<query>+when:2d&hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en (when:1h to when:7d limits age; widen to when:7d if the list is thin). Dismiss the "Before you continue" consent dialog; an "unusual traffic" page means `login`.
2. `read` the results: headline, outlet, age for the first 20. Note which outlets are primary reporters (AP, Reuters, local papers, the organisation itself) and which are rewrites.
3. If a result shows a "Full coverage" link, click it and `read` the story page: it groups "Top news", "Frequently asked questions" and "All coverage", and for big stories a timeline. Skip this if there is no Full coverage link.
4. Open the 4 most recent articles from different outlets (click the headline, it redirects to the outlet; close popups) and `read` the first screen of each. Take the date from the article, not from Google.
5. Report a dated timeline of developments (oldest to newest), the current state in two lines, what is still unknown or disputed, and the 4 links with outlet and date. Mark opinion pieces as such.
