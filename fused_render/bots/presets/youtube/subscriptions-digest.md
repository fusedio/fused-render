# YouTube subscriptions digest
trigger: my subscriptions, youtube subscriptions, what's new on youtube, youtube digest, new from my channels, subscription feed, youtube this week

1. goto https://www.youtube.com/feed/subscriptions . If a sign-in page appears, use `login`.
2. `read` the feed, then scroll 3 times and `read` again, collecting channel, title, length, views and age for each video.
3. Stop collecting once videos are older than the period in the task (default: last 2 days). Skip Shorts unless asked; note "LIVE", "Premiere" and "Members only" badges.
4. Group by channel. Open the 3 videos that best match the task (else the 3 longest from channels the user watches most), click "…more", `read` the description, then `back`.
5. Report grouped bullets, newest first: title, length, age, link; a two-line gist for the 3 you opened; call out anything over an hour, live or premiering. More than 15 videos: `save` the full list as youtube-subscriptions.md and keep the chat to the top 10.
