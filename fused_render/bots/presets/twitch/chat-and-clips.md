# Twitch chat and clips from a stream
trigger: what is twitch chat saying, read the chat, twitch clips, best clips, clips from, what happened on stream, stream highlights

1. goto https://www.twitch.tv/<channel>. If the channel is live, the chat column is on the right: `read` it, then `wait` 10 seconds and `read` it again, twice, to sample about a minute of messages. Dismiss "Welcome to the chat room" and any extension prompts. Never type in the chat box.
2. Note the recurring topics, emotes spam, questions to the streamer, and any pinned message or chat mode banner (followers-only, sub-only, slow mode).
3. goto https://www.twitch.tv/<channel>/clips?filter=clips&range=24h (or 7d, 30d per the task) and `read` the first 12 clips: title, views, who clipped, when. Open at most 3 top clips and `read` the title, category and chapter marker shown.
4. If the task asks about a past stream, goto https://www.twitch.tv/<channel>/videos?filter=archives&sort=time, open the matching VOD, and `read` the chapters list under the player and the stream title.
5. Report: a three-line summary of what chat is talking about with 5 representative messages (no usernames unless asked), the chat mode, and the top clips as bullets with views and links.
