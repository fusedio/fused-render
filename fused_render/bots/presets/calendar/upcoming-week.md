# Google Calendar week ahead
trigger: week ahead, next week, this week's schedule, upcoming events, upcoming travel, calendar this week, calendar next week, my week

1. goto https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r/week (`login` if a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears). For a later week press `n` once per week, or goto https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r/week/YYYY/M/D with that week's Monday.
2. `read` the week grid: the all-day row (holidays, out of office, travel) and each day's timed events. Scroll the grid so 7:00 and 20:00 are both seen; early and late events sit outside the first screen.
3. If the week has more than about 25 events, press `a` or goto https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r/agenda and `read` the list form instead; it is easier to read than the grid and shows the same events.
4. Click at most 4 events whose titles are unclear or that the task asks about and `read` the detail panel: description, place, guests and their responses. Close it with the panel's X.
5. If the task names a second week, repeat steps 1-3 for it; never read more than 3 weeks.
6. Do not create, move or answer anything. Report one list per day, Monday to Sunday, with time, title and place, then three lines: busiest day, free or nearly free days, and overlaps or unanswered invitations. More than 2 weeks: `save` the full listing as calendar-weeks.md and keep the chat answer to the highlights. End with the week-view URL.
