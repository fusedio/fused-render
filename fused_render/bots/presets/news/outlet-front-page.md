# News outlet front page check
trigger: front page of, bbc news, reuters, ap news, nytimes, new york times, washington post, wall street journal

1. goto the outlet's front page the task names: https://apnews.com/, https://www.reuters.com/, https://www.bbc.com/news, https://www.nytimes.com/, https://www.washingtonpost.com/, https://www.theguardian.com/us or https://www.wsj.com/. Close cookie and subscribe popups. Never sign in to a paywalled outlet unless the task says the user subscribes; then use `login`.
2. `read` the front page: the lead story block and the next 10-12 headlines in order, with their sections and any "Opinion" or "Analysis" labels.
3. If the task asks about a section (Business, Technology, World, Politics), click that nav link and `read` its first screen too.
4. Open the lead story and the two below it; `read` the first screen of each (paywalled: use the free paragraphs and say so).
5. Report: what the outlet led with and why it matters, then 10 bullets of headline, one-line gist and link, with opinion pieces listed separately. If the task compares two outlets, repeat for the second and end with a two-row table of what each led with.
