# Google Calendar today and tomorrow
trigger: my calendar, my schedule, what's on today, what do i have tomorrow, today's meetings, tomorrow's meetings, my day, calendar today

1. goto https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r/day. If a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`. Dismiss any "What's new" or feature-tip popup with its Got it or X button.
2. `read` the day grid: the all-day row at the top, then each timed event with start and end. Note the calendar each event belongs to and any event drawn as an outline only (an invitation I have not answered).
3. Press `n` (next) or click the ">" arrow beside "Today" to move to tomorrow and `read` it the same way; `p` or "<" goes back. If the key does nothing, an event panel is open: close it with its X and use the arrow.
4. Click at most 4 events whose titles are unclear, or that the task asks about, and `read` the detail panel: description, location or Google Meet link, organiser, and the guest list with Yes / No / Maybe / Awaiting counts. Close the panel with its X.
5. If the task names another day, goto https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r/day/YYYY/M/D for it. Never read more than 3 days in this playbook; a longer range is the "week ahead" playbook.
6. Do not accept, decline, move or create anything. Report two lists, Today and Tomorrow, in time order: time range, title, place or Meet link, who it is with. Flag back-to-back blocks, overlaps and unanswered invitations at the top, and end with the day-view URL for each day.
