# Slack channel or thread catch-up
trigger: summarize this thread, slack thread, what happened in, catch me up on, summarize the channel, recap the channel, what's the status in, slack channel summary

1. Open the conversation: goto the link if the task gives one; otherwise press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on Mac), type the channel or person name, and press Enter. `login` if a sign-in page, workspace picker, 2FA or captcha appears.
2. Decide the period: the one the task names, else since yesterday. If the task gives a thread link, `read` the whole thread in the right pane, scrolling up until the parent message is visible, then skip to step 6.
3. In the channel, scroll up until the date divider before the period start is on screen, then `read` the messages. Scroll down and `read` again until the newest message is visible. Stop at about 300 messages: if the period is longer, `save` what was read and say the summary covers part of it.
4. For messages with "N replies", click the reply count to open the thread on the right and `read` it; do this for the 5 threads with the most replies or that mention me. Close the pane with its X, not Escape.
5. Note reactions on key messages (a tick or thumbs-up often marks agreement) and any pinned message shown at the top of the channel.
6. Do not reply, react or mark anything. Report: decisions, open questions with owners, action items with names and dates, then a timeline of up to 10 key messages (date, person, one line) each with its permalink (the href of the message timestamp). Longer: `save` as slack-catchup.md and keep the chat answer to the three lists.
