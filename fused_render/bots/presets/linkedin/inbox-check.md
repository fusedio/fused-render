# LinkedIn notifications, messages and invitations
trigger: linkedin notifications, linkedin messages, linkedin inbox, linkedin invitations, message me on linkedin, linkedin dms, connection requests, pending invitations

1. goto https://www.linkedin.com/notifications/ and `wait` for the list. If a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`. Close the Messaging overlay first.
2. `read` the notification list: who, what (reaction, comment, mention, "viewed your profile", job alert, birthday) and when. Then click the "My posts" chip and the "Mentions" chip and `read` each: these hold the items that may need a reply.
3. goto https://www.linkedin.com/messaging/ . `read` the "Focused" tab list, then click "Other" and `read` it. Open at most the 3 newest unread threads and `read` each: sender, headline, last message in full. Opening marks them read; that is accepted. Never reply.
4. goto https://www.linkedin.com/mynetwork/ , click "Show all" beside Invitations (or "Manage") and `read` the received invitations: name, headline, mutual connections, any note. Never accept or ignore any.
5. Report three lists, newest first: Notifications (counts by type, plus every comment or mention on the user's posts quoted in one line with its link), Messages (sender, gist, needs a reply or not, thread link), Invitations (name, headline, note). End with up to 3 items that deserve an answer and a suggested one-line reply each; send nothing.
