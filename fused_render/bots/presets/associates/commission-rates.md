# Amazon Associates commission rate for a product
trigger: commission rate for, associates fee rate, how much commission on amazon, amazon affiliate rate, fee schedule, standard commission income, bounty

1. goto https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/schedule (the Commission Income Statement) and `read` the Fixed Standard Commission Income table: product category and rate.
2. If the task names a product, open it on https://www.amazon.com (search its name or ASIN) and `read` the category from the breadcrumb and the Product Details section; match it to the table's category. For a logged-out captcha page, use `login`.
3. On the same help page scroll to the Special Commission Income (bounty) table and `read` the fixed-fee events: Prime trials, Audible, Kindle Unlimited, Amazon Business and similar.
4. Note categories excluded or at a reduced rate (gift cards, wine, some digital items) from the exclusions list on the page.
5. Report: the category, the rate, what the fee would be at the product's current price, applicable bounties, and the link to the schedule. If the category is ambiguous, say which two rates could apply.
