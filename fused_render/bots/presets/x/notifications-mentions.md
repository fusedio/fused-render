# X notifications, mentions and DMs
trigger: x notifications, twitter notifications, my mentions, who mentioned me, x messages, x dms, replies to me on x, who replied to me

1. goto https://x.com/notifications/mentions and `read` the list: who replied or mentioned, their text, which of the user's posts it is on, time, link. Scroll twice. If a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`.
2. Click the "All" tab (or "Priority" if present: mutuals and large accounts) and `read` the first 2 screens: count likes, reposts, new followers and quotes by type instead of listing each.
3. goto https://x.com/messages and `read` the conversation list; open at most the 3 newest unread and `read` each (sender, last message in full). Never reply.
4. goto https://x.com/messages/requests and `read` the list of requests without opening any.
5. Report three lists, newest first: Mentions and replies (quoted in one line each, with links), Activity (counts by type plus notable accounts), Messages (sender, gist, needs a reply or not, plus the count of requests). End with up to 3 items that deserve an answer and a suggested one-line reply each; send nothing.
