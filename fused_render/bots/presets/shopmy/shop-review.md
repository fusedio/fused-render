# ShopMy shop review
trigger: my shopmy shop, review my shop, shopmy collections, check my shelves, what's on my shopmy, shopmy sections, out of stock on shopmy, my shopmy shelves

1. goto https://shopmy.us/<username> (the public shop; no login needed). If the username is unknown, open the dashboard "My Shop" page first (use `login` if asked), copy the shop URL from the address bar, then open it.
2. `read` the Section tabs across the top; open each Section and `read` its collections and products: name, retailer, price. Scroll each collection to the end.
3. Pick 10 products to check: those the task names, else the first 10 in the newest collections. Open each product's retailer page with tab new, `read` price and availability, tab close. Note sold out, price changed, 404, or a redirect to the retailer's home page.
4. If the task asks about empty, hidden or archived collections, open the dashboard "My Shop" editor and `read` the collection list; do not click "Edit", "Add Collection", "+ Product", archive or unarchive.
5. Report section by section: collection, product count, then a table of the checked products (product, retailer, shop price, live price, status). Put sold-out and broken first, suggest a replacement from the same brand for each, and give the shop link and each collection link. More than 25 products? `save` as shopmy-shop-review.csv.
