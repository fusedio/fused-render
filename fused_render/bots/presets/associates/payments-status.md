# Amazon Associates payments and account status
trigger: associates payment, when do i get paid, affiliate payout, associates payment history, account status, associates minimum payout, tax interview

1. goto https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/home/account/payment (Account Settings > Payment History). If a sign-in page appears, use `login`. Change nothing on the Change Payment Method page.
2. `read` the payment history: date, amount, method (direct deposit, gift card, check), status, for the last 6 payments.
3. goto https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/home/account and `read` the account summary: store ID, payment method on file, tax information status, and any warning about the 180-day qualifying sales requirement or site review.
4. goto https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/home/reports, set the current month, and `read` Total Fees to show what is accruing for the next payment (paid about 60 days after month end, once the minimum for the method is met).
5. Report the last payments as a table, the accrued fees this month, the expected next payment date, and any account warning with its text. Never edit payment, tax or address details; if the task asks for that, stop at the form and show the user what would change.
