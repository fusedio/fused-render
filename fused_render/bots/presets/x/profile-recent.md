# X profile recent posts
trigger: recent posts from, x profile, twitter profile, latest from, what did they post on x, posts by, check their x, their tweets

1. goto https://x.com/<handle> . Profiles need login: if a sign-in page or captcha appears, use `login`. `read` the header: bio, location, link, joined date, following and followers, pinned post.
2. Stay on the "Posts" tab (the "Replies", "Media" and "Articles" tabs are separate). Scroll 4 times, reading posts newest first with time and engagement, until they are older than the task's period (default 7 days). Reposts carry a "reposted" label.
3. Click "Show more" on long posts; open at most 3 threads the person started ("Show this thread") and `read` them, `back` after each.
4. If the task names a topic, also goto https://x.com/search?q=from%3A<handle>%20<topic>&src=typed_query&f=live and `read` the first screen, so older on-topic posts are not missed.
5. Report: a profile line (bio, followers, posting rate), then posts newest first with date, gist, engagement and link; flag announcements, launches, job changes and replies to bigger threads. More than 10 posts: `save` as x-profile.md.
