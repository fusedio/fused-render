# Indeed research one job posting
trigger: this job on indeed, is this job legit, tell me about this posting, research this job, should i apply to, indeed posting, is this a good job

1. goto the posting link (https://www.indeed.com/viewjob?jk=<id>); if the task gives only a title and company, search https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=<title>+<company>&l=<city> and click the matching card. Cloudflare → `login`.
2. `read` the whole description: duties, must-have requirements, pay line and benefits, job type and schedule, posted age, whether the button says "Apply now" or "Apply on company site", and the company rating beside its name.
3. Click the company name to open https://www.indeed.com/cmp/<Company> and `read`: rating with review count, Work wellbeing score, size, industry, headquarters; then the "Reviews" tab's 10 most recent reviews (role, date, pros, cons).
4. Open the company's "Salaries" tab and read the figure for this title; compare it with the posting's pay. Open the "Interviews" tab and read the difficulty rating and up to 3 reported questions if present.
5. Check red flags: no company page, pay far above the salary figure, vague duties, an external apply link on a domain that is not the company's, the same posting repeated under several locations. Never click "Apply now".
6. Report: the role in five lines (duties, requirements, pay, type, posted), the company in three lines (rating, size, what recent reviews say), a fit or red-flag verdict, and links to the posting, company page and salaries.
