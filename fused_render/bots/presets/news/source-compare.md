# Google News compare outlets' coverage
trigger: how are outlets covering, compare coverage, different takes on, left and right coverage, how is the press reporting, media coverage of, framing of

1. goto https://news.google.com/search?q=<story>+when:3d&hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en; dismiss the consent dialog; captcha → `login`. `read` the result list and click the story's "Full coverage" link.
2. `read` the Full coverage page: it lists the same story from many outlets. Pick 4 across the spectrum (for example AP or Reuters, one national broadsheet, one cable or tabloid outlet, one specialist or foreign outlet). If the task names outlets, use those.
3. Open each of the 4 (click the headline, close cookie or subscribe popups) and `read` the headline, subheading and first screen. Note which fact leads, who is quoted, what is omitted, loaded words, and whether it is labelled opinion or analysis.
4. If an outlet is paywalled, use what loads and say so; do not try to get around it.
5. Report a table: outlet, headline, lead fact, quoted sources, what it leaves out; then the facts all 4 agree on; then the sharpest disagreement; a link for each.
