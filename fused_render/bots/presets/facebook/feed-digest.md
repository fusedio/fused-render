# Facebook feed digest (most recent)
trigger: facebook feed, my facebook, what's new on facebook, facebook digest, catch me up on facebook, what did my friends post on facebook, facebook today

1. goto https://www.facebook.com/?sk=h_chr (the Feeds > Most recent view: friends, groups and pages, no suggestions). For friends only goto https://www.facebook.com/?filter=friends&sk=h_chr . If it bounces to the home feed, click Feeds in the left menu, then Most recent. On a sign-in page, 2FA or checkpoint use `login`.
2. Close the "Turn on notifications" prompt and any Messenger chat bubbles with their X.
3. `read` the feed, then scroll 4 times and `read` after each: who posted (person, Page or group name), date, text, reaction count, comment and share counts. Skip Sponsored, Suggested for you and Reels rows. Click "See more" on a truncated post worth reading.
4. Pick 10 posts: people or groups the task names first, then by engagement. Open at most 3 (click the comment count) and `read` the top comments; Escape closes the dialog.
5. `read` the right column for "Birthdays" today and the "Contacts" who are online only if the task asks about birthdays.
6. Report 10 bullets newest first: who, one-line gist, reactions/comments, link (the post's timestamp is its link). End with a birthdays line if any. Over 15 posts: `save` facebook-feed.md and keep the chat to 8.
