# X timeline digest
trigger: my timeline, x timeline, twitter timeline, what's on x, what's on twitter, x digest, catch up on x, home feed on x

1. goto https://x.com/home . If a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`. Click the "Following" tab (chronological) unless the task wants "For you". Press Escape on any Premium or "Keep up with" dialog.
2. Scroll down 5 times. On each screen note the author @handle, text, time, and replies, reposts, likes and views. Skip posts labelled "Ad" and the "Who to follow" and "What's happening" modules.
3. Click "Show more" on truncated posts that match the task's topic; open a thread (click the post) only if its first post is on topic, `read` it, then `back`. Open at most 3.
4. If "Rate limit exceeded" or "Something went wrong" appears, `wait`, retry once, then report what you have.
5. Pick the 10 most relevant posts (task topic first, then by engagement).
6. Report 10 bullets: @handle, one-line gist, engagement, time, link (x.com/<handle>/status/<id>, from the timestamp's href). Group by topic if more than three topics appear. More than 10 asked for: `save` as x-timeline.md.
