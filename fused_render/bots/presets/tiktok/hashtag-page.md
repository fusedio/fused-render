# TikTok hashtag page
trigger: tiktok hashtag, how big is #, tiktok tag, posts under the hashtag, tiktok.com/tag, videos with the hashtag on tiktok

1. goto https://www.tiktok.com/tag/<tag> (no # in the URL). On a captcha, sign-in sheet or blank page use `login`, then goto again.
2. `read` the header: the hashtag and its post count. TikTok removed total view counts from tag pages in January 2024; for views goto https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/hashtag/<tag>/pc/en (no login needed) and `read` posts, views and the trend line.
3. `read` the grid: creator, caption snippet and play count per tile; scroll once for about 30 tiles. The order is algorithmic, not chronological, so take dates from each video.
4. Open 4 tiles (the 2 most viewed and the 2 that fit the task best): `read` caption, sound, date, counts and 5 comments. Escape closes the video.
5. Report: post count (and views from Creative Center), creators that appear more than once, the common angle of the videos, and 5 links with counts. If the page shows "No results" for a tag that clearly exists, say it is restricted or throttled and `remember` that.
