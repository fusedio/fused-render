# YouTube channel latest uploads
trigger: latest videos from, channel uploads, new videos from, what did they upload, youtube channel, uploaded recently, recent videos on their channel

1. goto https://www.youtube.com/@<handle>/videos . If you only have a name, goto https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=<name> , click "Filters" > Type > "Channel", open the channel, then its "Videos" tab.
2. Click the "Latest" chip (not "Popular" or "Oldest"; the desktop site remembers the last choice). `read` the grid: 12 cards with title, length, views, age. Use the "Live" or "Shorts" tab instead if the task asks for streams or Shorts.
3. Click "…more" in the channel header and `read` the About dialog: subscribers, total videos, joined date, links. Press Escape.
4. Open the 3 newest videos (or those matching the task). For each click "…more", `read` the description, click "Show transcript" and `read` the first screen, then `back`.
5. Report: one channel line (subscribers, upload cadence judged from the ages in the grid), then the 12 videos newest first with title, length, views, age and link, with a two-line gist for the 3 you opened.
