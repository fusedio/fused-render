# Hacker News best, newest and past days
trigger: best of hn, hn this week, hn newest, new on hn, hn last week, what was on hn on, hn yesterday, rising on hn

1. Pick the list: https://news.ycombinator.com/best for the highest-voted stories of the last few days, https://news.ycombinator.com/newest for brand-new submissions, https://news.ycombinator.com/active for the most active discussions. `read` the table: title, domain, points, comments, age. On /newest, items under 30 minutes old have few points yet, so give the age with each.
2. For a past day goto https://news.ycombinator.com/front?day=YYYY-MM-DD (add &p=2 for rows 31-60) and `read` it. Use it for "yesterday", and one page per day for "last week" (at most 7 pages). The /front rows show the final points, so they rank well.
3. Pick 8 stories by the task's topic, else by points. Across several days, take the top 8 overall, not 8 per day.
4. Open the 8 comments pages (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=<id>) and `read` the top of each thread for the argument of the top two root comments. At most 8 thread pages; skip the articles unless a title is unclear.
5. Report 8 bullets with a one-line gist, what the comments argue, points, comment count, HN link and article link; when several days are covered use a table with a day column instead. For a week's worth, `save` the full table as hn-week.md and keep the chat answer to the top 10 by points.
