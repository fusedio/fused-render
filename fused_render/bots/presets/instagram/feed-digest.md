# Instagram home feed digest
trigger: instagram feed, my instagram, what's new on instagram, instagram digest, catch me up on instagram, instagram following feed, what did my friends post on instagram

1. goto https://www.instagram.com/?variant=following (the Following feed: only accounts you follow, newest first, no suggestions). If it lands on the default home feed, click the Instagram wordmark top-left and choose Following from its dropdown. On a sign-in page or challenge use `login`.
2. Dismiss "Turn on notifications" with Not Now. Ignore the Stories bar unless the task asks who posted a story (stories cannot be read, only the account names).
3. `read` the feed, then scroll 4 times and `read` after each: account, time (hover the timestamp for the exact date), caption, like and comment counts. Skip "Sponsored" and "Suggested for you" cards.
4. Collect 10 posts: accounts the task names first, then newest. Open at most 3 whose comments matter and `read` the top comments; Escape closes them.
5. Report 10 bullets newest first: account, one-line gist from caption and alt text, likes/comments, link https://www.instagram.com/p/<code>/. Add one line naming accounts with a new story. Over 15 posts: `save` instagram-feed.md and keep the chat to 8.
