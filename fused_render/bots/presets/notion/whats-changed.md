# Notion what changed
trigger: what changed in notion, notion updates, recently edited in notion, notion activity, what did the team update, notion inbox, notion notifications, notion version history

1. goto https://www.notion.so and click "Inbox" at the top of the left sidebar. If a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`.
2. Set the Inbox filter: read status "Unread and read" (or "All workspace updates" when the task wants everything, not just things aimed at me) and the types the task cares about (Mentions, Discussions, Updates, Access). `read` the list: page, who, kind of change, when. Do not click "Archive read" or "Archive all".
3. If the task is about one page rather than the Inbox, open that page, click the "•••" menu at the top right and choose "Version history": the right-hand list shows versions by date and editor; click a version to see the highlighted changes. Never click "Restore".
4. Open the 5 most relevant pages from the Inbox (clicking a notification jumps to the changed block) and `read` the changed section. Where the exact diff matters, use "Version history" as in step 3 and compare the latest two versions.
5. Keep to the period the task names (default: since yesterday). Version history only reaches back 7 days on Free, 30 on Plus and 90 on Business; say so if the task asks for longer.
6. Report page by page, newest first: page, who changed it, a one-line gist of the change, when, and the link. More than 10 pages? `save` the full list as notion-changes.md and keep the chat to the top 5.
