# Google News morning digest
trigger: news digest, morning news, what's in the news, today's headlines, news briefing, top stories today, google news

1. goto https://news.google.com/home?hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en. If a "Before you continue to Google" consent dialog appears, click "Reject all" (or "Accept all"); if Google shows an "unusual traffic" captcha page, use `login`.
2. `read` the Home page: the "Top stories" cluster (headline, outlet, age) and the "Your topics" and "Local news" blocks below it. Each story cluster carries a "Full coverage" link.
3. For the sections the task names (default: Top stories, then the "Business" and "Technology" tabs in the top nav next to Home, For you, Following) click the tab and `read` the first screen: about 10 headlines each.
4. Pick the 8 most important stories across sections, favouring ones several outlets cover. Open each lead article by clicking its headline (it redirects to the outlet), close any cookie or subscribe popup, and `read` the first screen. If a paywall shows only a paragraph, use that and say so. Open no more than 8 articles.
5. Report 8 bullets grouped by section: what happened, why it matters, outlet, date, article link; note where outlets disagree. If the user asked for more than 10 stories, `save` the full list as news-digest.md and keep the chat to the top 5.
