# LTK shop review
trigger: my ltk posts, review my ltk shop, ltk collections, check my ltk, what's on my ltk, my ltk shop, sold out on ltk, ltk broken links

1. goto https://www.shopltk.com/explore/<handle> (public; no login). If the handle is unknown, open https://creator.shopltk.com (use `login` if asked), read the shop link in the profile or account settings, and come back.
2. Scroll until the last 10 posts are visible and `read` them: date, caption, number of linked products. Open the newest 10 posts (or the ones the task names) one by one and `read` the product list under each: product, retailer, price, any sold-out label.
3. For each post, open at most 3 products' retailer pages with tab new, `read` price and availability, tab close. Prioritise products the caption calls out. Note sold out, price up more than 20%, 404, or a redirect to a category page.
4. If the task is about a draft or scheduled post, open creator.shopltk.com and the Posts / Publish area and `read` it; do not click Publish, Edit or Delete.
5. Report post by post, newest first: date, caption gist, products checked with status; then the sold-out and broken items first with a replacement suggestion from the same retailer; links to the shop and each post. More than 10 posts? `save` as ltk-shop-review.csv.
