# Amazon Associates earnings digest
trigger: associates earnings, affiliate earnings, amazon associates, associates report, how much did my links make, amazon affiliate income, associates dashboard

1. goto https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/home. If a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`. Dismiss any announcement banner.
2. `read` the Summary on the home page: Total Clicks, Ordered Items, Shipped Items, Conversion, Total Earnings for the default period.
3. goto https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/home/reports and set the date range the task names (default: last 7 days) with the date picker at the top; click the Earnings (Fees) tab and `read` the totals: Ordered Items, Shipped Items, Returned Items, Total Fees, and any bonus or bounty line.
4. Click the Orders tab and `read` the first 20 rows: product, ASIN, qty, price, tracking ID, date. Note returns.
5. Click the Tracking IDs tab and `read` clicks, orders, conversion and fees per tracking ID.
6. Report: the period totals with the change versus the previous period (set the range once more if the task asks for a comparison), the top 5 products by fees, the tracking IDs ranked by fees, and anything returned. If more than 10 rows matter, `save` them as associates-report.csv.
