# Facebook group: new posts
trigger: facebook group, posts in the group, what's new in the group, facebook group feed, group posts, in my facebook group, the group on facebook

1. goto the group URL from the task with ?sorting_setting=CHRONOLOGICAL appended (https://www.facebook.com/groups/<id-or-slug>/?sorting_setting=CHRONOLOGICAL) so the feed is newest first. No URL given: goto https://www.facebook.com/search/groups/?q=<name>, `read` the results (name, members, Joined badge), open the right one, then add the sort parameter. On a sign-in page or checkpoint use `login`.
2. A private group showing a "Join group" button means the user is not a member: report that and stop; never join.
3. Check the sort control under the composer reads "New posts"; if it says "Most relevant", click it and pick New posts (the third option, New activity, sorts by latest comment).
4. `read` the feed and scroll 3 times, `read` after each, collecting posts from the period the task names (default: last 2 days): author, date, text, reactions, comment count. Click "See more" on long posts.
5. Open the 3 most commented posts (click the comment count) and `read` the top comments; Escape closes the dialog.
6. If the task asks about rules or pinned content, `read` the Featured section at the top of the feed and the About tab in the group header.
7. Report posts newest first: author, gist, reactions/comments, link. Call out questions, items for sale, events and admin announcements. Over 12 posts: `save` facebook-group-<name>.md and keep the chat to 8.
