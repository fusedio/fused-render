# YouTube comments and reactions on a video
trigger: comments on this video, what do people say about the video, youtube comments, viewer reactions, comments section, top comments on youtube, how was the video received

1. goto the video URL (or search its title at https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=<title> and open the first match). Click "Reject all" if the consent dialog shows.
2. `read` the title block (title, channel, views, date) and the like count under the player.
3. Scroll down twice until the comments load. `read` the comments section with "Sort by" at its default "Top comments": author, text, likes, reply count. Scroll twice more to collect about 30 comments.
4. Click "Sort by" and pick "Newest first"; `read` the first 10 for the current mood.
5. Expand the replies ("N replies") under the 3 most-liked comments and `read` them.
6. Report: like and comment counts; 3 to 5 recurring themes with one representative quote each (author, likes); the strongest disagreement; any corrections or timestamps the comments point to; the video link.
