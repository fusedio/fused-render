# Notion read a public page
trigger: notion.site, public notion page, this notion link, notion page at, read this notion, notion doc link, published notion, summarize this notion

1. goto the URL from the task. Pages on *.notion.site, custom Notion Sites domains and notion.so links shared with "Anyone on the web with link" open without login; do not use `login` unless a sign-in wall actually appears.
2. Dismiss any "Duplicate" prompt or cookie banner. Scroll to the end so every block loads, then `read` the page. Toggle blocks and collapsed headings hide text: click each "▶" before reading.
3. If the page links to subpages or shows a sidebar of pages, open the 3 most relevant to the task and `read` each; use `back` to return.
4. If the page embeds a database, scroll until "Load more" is gone and `read` it as a table.
5. Report: the page title and site, a summary in the shape the task asks for (bullets by default), key passages quoted exactly, and links to the page and each subpage you opened. Longer than a chat message? `save` the full text as notion-<title>.md and summarise in chat.
