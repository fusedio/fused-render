# Notion page views and access
trigger: who viewed my notion, notion page analytics, who has access in notion, notion sharing, who can see this notion page, notion page views, notion permissions, shared too widely

1. Open the page from the task (URL, or Search with Control+k). If a sign-in page, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`.
2. Click "Share" at the top right and `read` the Share tab: each person or group with the permission level in the dropdown next to their name, and the "General access" value ("Only people invited", "Everyone at <workspace>", or "Anyone on the web with link"). Click the "Publish" tab and note whether it is published and the public URL. Do not invite anyone, change a permission or click "Publish"; press Escape to close.
3. Click the "•••" menu at the top right and choose "Updates & analytics"; open the "Analytics" tab. `read` total views and unique views (hover the graph for a date) and the "Viewers" list with last-viewed times. Then `read` the "Updates" tab for who edited what and when. This needs owner or editor rights; if the tab is missing, say so.
4. If the task covers several pages, repeat for at most 4.
5. Report per page: who has access and at what level, the general access and publish state, total and unique views, the last 5 viewers with dates, the last 5 edits, and the link. Flag anything shared wider than the task expects. `remember` it if the workspace has page analytics switched off.
