# Gmail newsletters and subscriptions
trigger: newsletters, unsubscribe, who keeps emailing me, promotions tab, mailing lists, manage subscriptions, too many emails, clean up my inbox

1. goto https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#inbox ; on a sign-in page use `login`. In the left navigation click "More", then "Manage subscriptions": the page lists every sender you are subscribed to, grouped by sender, sorted by how often they email, each with an Unsubscribe button.
2. `read` the list: sender, how many emails recently, the latest subject. If the account has no Manage subscriptions entry yet, goto https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#search/unsubscribe+newer_than%3A30d and https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#category/promotions and `read` the first page of each, counting rows per sender.
3. For the 5 senders with the most mail open one message each and `read` what they send and whether an "Unsubscribe" link sits next to the sender name in the header.
4. Clicking Unsubscribe, Block or Report spam is risky: do not do it unless the task names the senders. If it does, click Unsubscribe for each named sender, treat the confirmation dialog as the risky step, and stop after the named ones.
5. Report a table: sender, emails per month, kind (promo, update, newsletter, social), what they send, suggested keep or unsubscribe. Over 15 rows: `save` gmail-subscriptions.md and keep the chat to the top 10.
