# YouTube playlist or Watch Later rundown
trigger: my watch later, watch later, youtube playlist, what's in the playlist, youtube history, my liked videos, what did i watch

1. goto https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=WL for Watch Later, https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=LL for Liked videos, https://www.youtube.com/feed/history for watch history, or the playlist URL from the task. If a sign-in page appears, use `login`.
2. `read` the list: position, title, channel, length, age. Scroll twice to load up to 40 items. Note the playlist header (video count, total length if shown).
3. Open the 3 items the task cares about (default: the 3 newest additions), click "…more" and `read` the description, then "Show transcript" and `read` its first screen; `back` after each.
4. Never remove, reorder or add videos, and never clear history.
5. Report a table (title, channel, length, link) with a two-line gist for the 3 you opened, plus the total count and summed length. More than 15 items: `save` as youtube-playlist.csv and show the top 10.
