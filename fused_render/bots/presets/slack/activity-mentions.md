# Slack mentions and activity
trigger: slack mentions, who mentioned me, slack activity, replies to me on slack, slack reactions, slack threads waiting, waiting on me on slack

1. goto https://app.slack.com/client and `login` if a sign-in page, workspace picker, 2FA or captcha appears. Click "Activity" in the left rail (Ctrl+Shift+M; Cmd+Shift+M on Mac).
2. `read` the Activity list. Narrow it with the tabs at the top: "Mentions" for @me and @channel pings, "Threads" for replies in threads I am in, "Reactions" for emoji on my messages, "DMs". Switch on the "Unreads" toggle at the top when the task only wants what is new. Read at most 25 items.
3. For each Mention or Thread item that contains a question or request (at most 6), click it: the message opens in the main pane with its thread on the right. `read` the thread so the answer says what was asked and whether someone already replied.
4. Click "Threads" at the top of the Home sidebar (Ctrl+Shift+T; Cmd+Shift+T on Mac) and `read` the first screen for threads with new replies that Activity did not list.
5. Do not reply, react or clear anything. In Activity the keys R and C mark items read or clear them, so never type while this view is focused. The permalink of a message is the href of its timestamp link (…/archives/C…/p…); take it from the element, since "Copy link" writes to a clipboard you cannot read. `remember` that if it was not known.
6. Report a table: channel, who, when, what they asked or said in one line, and status (needs my reply / already answered / FYI), with the ones needing a reply first and a permalink for the top 5. More than 15 rows: `save` as slack-activity.md.
