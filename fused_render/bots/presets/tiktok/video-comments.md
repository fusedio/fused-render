# TikTok video: caption and comments
trigger: this tiktok, tiktok video, comments on tiktok, tiktok.com/@, what are people saying about this tiktok, tiktok link, summarise this tiktok

1. goto the video URL (https://www.tiktok.com/@<handle>/video/<id>; a vm.tiktok.com short link redirects there). On a captcha, sign-in sheet or blank page use `login`, then goto again.
2. `read` the right panel: creator, date, caption with hashtags and mentions, the sound title ("original sound - <name>" or a song), likes, comments, bookmarks, shares. The video cannot be watched; if a "CC" captions toggle is shown, click it and `read` the on-screen caption text as it appears.
3. `read` the comment panel; scroll it at most 3 times and click "View N replies" on the 3 most-liked comments. Collect up to 15: author, text, likes, time, and replies marked "Creator".
4. Click the creator's handle, `read` follower count and bio for context, then `back`.
5. Report: the video line (creator, date, caption gist, sound, counts, link), what the comments argue in 3-4 themes with 5 quoted examples, and the answer to the task's question. Over 20 comments: `save` tiktok-comments.md.
