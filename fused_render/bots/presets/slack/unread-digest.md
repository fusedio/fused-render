# Slack unread digest
trigger: slack digest, what's new on slack, unread slack, catch me up on slack, slack unreads, what did i miss on slack, slack summary

1. goto https://app.slack.com/client. If a sign-in page, workspace picker, 2FA or captcha appears, use `login`; if an "Open Slack?" prompt appears, click "use Slack in your browser". If the task names a workspace, pick it in the workspace switcher on the far left.
2. Click "Unreads" at the top of the Home sidebar (or press Ctrl+Shift+A; Cmd+Shift+A on Mac). If it is not listed, open the "More" menu in the left rail and choose Unreads.
3. `read` the Unreads view: it stacks every conversation with unread messages. Use the "Sort" menu at the top to pick "Newest activity" if it is in another order. Do not click "Mark as Read" or "Mark All Messages Read", and do not press Escape.
4. For messages that mention me or ask a question and show "N replies", click the reply count to open the thread on the right and `read` it; at most 5 threads. Close the thread pane with its X.
5. If Unreads is empty, say so and instead `read` the first screen of up to 5 channels in the Starred section (or the first 5 in the sidebar).
6. Report channel by channel in the order shown, one line per message (who, gist, time), with three lists at the top: decisions, questions aimed at me, and deadlines or dates. More than 10 conversations: `save` the full digest as slack-unreads.md and keep the chat answer to the top 5. Quote the channel name with each item.
